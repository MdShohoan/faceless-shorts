# Inside Out

**Purpose:** simple anatomy, object mechanics, and process explainers.

**Visual language:** flat 2D diagrams, clean cutaways, x-ray-inspired translucent layers, arrows, labels, and limited color coding.

**Motion:** layer reveal, slide-in labels, animated arrows, simple shape transformations.

**Typography:** friendly sans-serif, short labels, generous spacing.

**Editorial rule:** educational only, not diagnosis or personal medical advice. Avoid frightening or graphic body imagery.
