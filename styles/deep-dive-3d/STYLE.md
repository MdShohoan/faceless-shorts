# Deep Dive 3D

**Purpose:** fast, curious science and everyday-phenomenon explainers.

**Visual language:** bold saturated colors, simple original 3D-inspired objects, soft studio lighting, strong depth cues, close-up macro shots, clean backgrounds, readable silhouettes.

**Motion:** quick establishing shot, slow push-ins, orbiting objects, cutaway transitions, restrained particle effects. Use a visual change every 1–3 seconds.

**Typography:** large condensed sans-serif, high contrast, 1–5 words per caption beat.

**Avoid:** copying another channel's exact models, compositions, branding, or signature transitions. Keep science claims accurate and clearly qualified.
