# Night File 3D

**Purpose:** respectful mystery, investigation, and historical-case explainers.

**Visual language:** dark navy/charcoal rooms, pools of warm practical light, evidence-board motifs, silhouettes, maps, clocks, paper files, rain-on-window textures.

**Motion:** slow dolly, parallax layers, controlled rack-focus simulation, gentle light flicker. Build suspense without graphic imagery.

**Typography:** restrained uppercase labels, clear subtitles, limited red/orange accents.

**Editorial rule:** distinguish documented facts from theories. Do not imply guilt or certainty where evidence is unresolved. Avoid graphic crime details.
