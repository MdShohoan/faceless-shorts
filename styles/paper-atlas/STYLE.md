# Paper Atlas

**Purpose:** history, geography, architecture, and timelines.

**Visual language:** layered cut-paper shapes, textured maps, hand-cut edges, paper buildings, labels, and timeline ribbons.

**Motion:** paper layers slide, fold, stack, and reveal; map routes draw on screen.

**Typography:** editorial serif for titles with a clean sans-serif for captions.

**Editorial rule:** date and source historical claims. Avoid invented quotations and misleading map boundaries.
