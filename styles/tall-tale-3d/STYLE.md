# Tall Tale 3D

**Purpose:** light, funny true stories and unusual historical anecdotes.

**Visual language:** bright sunny environments, flat cartoon characters placed in simple 3D-inspired sets, expressive but original faces, playful props.

**Motion:** squash-and-stretch used sparingly, comedic pauses, clear staging, gentle camera moves.

**Typography:** bold rounded sans-serif, short punchlines.

**Editorial rule:** the story must be verifiably true if presented as true. Label folklore or disputed accounts accurately.
