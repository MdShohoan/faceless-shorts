# Stick Story

**Purpose:** practical, non-hazardous preparedness and everyday decision stories.

**Visual language:** original stick-figure characters, minimal props, clear environments, simple iconography, a small consistent palette.

**Motion:** pose-to-pose character animation, pan between steps, simple diagrams and checklists.

**Typography:** large, direct action labels.

**Editorial rule:** keep advice conservative, age-appropriate, and aligned with official safety guidance. Never frame risky stunts or dangerous challenges as instructions.
